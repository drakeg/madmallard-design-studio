# Product and commercialization hypothesis

## Target customer
Small agencies and SMB marketing teams that need simple reusable branded graphics without a complex enterprise design suite. This is a **hypothesis**, not validated demand.

## Job to be done
Create and revise branded social media graphics and simple campaign assets quickly, without vendor lock-in or complex onboarding. The initial editor must support reliable create/edit/save/reopen/export workflows before a paid pilot.

## MVP acceptance criteria
- Create a document and add, select, move and resize rectangles, ellipses and text.
- Undo/redo, layers, basic typography, brand colors, save/open and PNG/SVG export.
- Same basic editing workflow in hosted web preview and downloadable macOS app; document any native-only functionality.
- Run a repeatable 10-minute user test with no data loss and no broken navigation.

## Commercial validation
Recruit five independent SMB users, observe task completion and measure time-to-first-export, task failures, and willingness to pay. Do not claim these interviews or metrics occurred until recorded. Pricing **hypothesis**: free local editor; $9–$19 per seat/month for optional hosted collaboration or managed templates, subject to demand and hosting-cost validation. Do not require a paid service to use local-first editing.

## Competition and positioning
Alternatives include Canva, Figma and Inkscape. Differentiate on fast, simple SMB brand assets, local-first ownership and an affordable optional service; verify whether this is compelling through actual pilots.

## Milestones
1. Working web demo and installable alpha with explicit signing status.
2. End-to-end editable project, persistence and export.
3. Five observed SMB trials, issue triage and pricing interviews.
4. Paid pilot only after evidence of willingness to pay and clear support scope.
